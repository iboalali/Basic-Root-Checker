Basic Root Checker 2.6 is out: four new languages, more accurate root manager detection, and animated result icons.

Basic Root Checker shows whether an Android device has root access, and which root manager grants it: Magisk, KernelSU or APatch.

New in 2.6:

• Four new languages: Dutch, Chinese (Simplified), Malay and Tamil. Nine in total.
• KernelSU (including KernelSU Next and SukiSU Ultra) and APatch are reported as themselves, not as Magisk.
• A device that already granted root access is reported as rooted on the first check after launch, still without a superuser prompt.
• Each result has its own animated icon: check, cross, question mark, or exclamation mark when root is installed but access wasn't granted.
• The update card no longer hangs on "Downloading" after a decline, a cancel, or a download that finished in the background.
• Request Root access asks again after a denied prompt, without restarting the app.
• Dialogs stay open through rotation, folding and resizing.
• The language picker scrolls, so every language can be chosen in landscape or with large text.
• Links no longer close the app on a device without a web browser.

Minimum version is now Android 7.0.

No ads. Collects no personal information.
